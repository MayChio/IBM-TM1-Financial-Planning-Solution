# Business Case

## Project Objective

The objective of this project was to modernize the financial planning, budgeting, and forecasting process using IBM Planning Analytics (TM1), Planning Analytics Workspace (PAW), Cognos Analytics, and automated TurboIntegrator processes.

## Business Need

The financial planning process required a more structured and automated solution to improve reporting, forecasting, data validation, and planning efficiency.

## Scope

The solution included:

- Financial planning
- Budgeting
- Forecasting
- OPEX analysis
- Workforce planning
- General Ledger validation
- Automated data loading
- Reporting and dashboards

## My Responsibilities

My responsibilities included:

- TM1 model design
- Cube development
- Dimension and hierarchy development
- Business Rules and Feeders
- TurboIntegrator automation
- Performance optimization
- Data validation
- Documentation
- Reporting and dashboard development

## Result

The solution reduced reporting time by approximately 30%, improved forecasting accuracy, and allowed business users to plan faster and more reliably.
